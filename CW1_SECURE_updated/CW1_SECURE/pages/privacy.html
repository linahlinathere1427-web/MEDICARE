Privacy Policy

Note: this is a coursework project. All data in it is example (synthetic) data and no real patient information is stored.

What data we collect

Name, email address, address, telephone number, date of birth, NHS number, allergies, appointments, prescription requests, medicines and clinical notes.

Why we collect it

Only to run the surgery service: booking appointments, requesting prescriptions and letting doctors keep medical notes. It is not used for marketing or shared with anyone else.

Who can see it

Patients see their own information and the notes a doctor chooses to share. Doctors can see patient records and notes. Admins manage patient details. Private doctor notes are not shown to patients.

How it is protected

Passwords and sensitive fields (such as telephone, address, date of birth, allergies and notes) are encrypted with AES-256 before being stored. Form input is checked and sanitised. Accounts are password protected, and repeated failed logins cause a temporary lock.

Where it is stored

In the browser database (IndexedDB) on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server.

How long we keep it

Until the record is deleted by an admin, or the browser data is cleared.

Your rights

You can ask the surgery admin to show you your data, correct it, delete it or give you a copy of it.
